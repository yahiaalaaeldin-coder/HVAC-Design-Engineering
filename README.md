# 🏢 HVAC Design Workflow

The general design workflow followed in my studies is:

```text
Building Information
        ↓
Room / Space Data
        ↓
External & Internal Loads
        ↓
Infiltration & Ventilation
        ↓
Cooling Load Calculation
        ↓
Airflow Requirements
        ↓
Duct & Air Distribution
        ↓
AHU / FCU / Chiller Selection
        ↓
Final HVAC Design
```
